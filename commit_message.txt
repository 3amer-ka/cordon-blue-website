🔒 Fix missing sandbox attribute on Google Maps iframe

🎯 What: Added `sandbox="allow-scripts allow-same-origin"` to the Google Maps iframe in `contact.html`.
⚠️ Risk: If left unfixed, embedded third-party content without sandbox restrictions could execute scripts with the same privileges as the parent page, potentially leading to Cross-Site Scripting (XSS), phishing, or clickjacking if the third-party source is compromised.
🛡️ Solution: The `sandbox` attribute restricts the capabilities of the embedded content, while `allow-scripts` and `allow-same-origin` are sufficient to maintain map functionality while minimizing risk.
