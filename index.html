 About Us

Excellence in Every Foundation

Cordon Blue Global Services Ltd is a premier construction and project management firm headquartered in Lagos, Nigeria. Founded in 2005, we have grown into a trusted partner for enterprises that demand precision, accountability and on-time delivery. Our multidisciplinary team brings decades of combined expertise in civil engineering, structural design, project management and high-end architectural solutions.

We built our reputation on critical energy and telecom infrastructure — CNG stations for Gastech and MTN Nigeria — and have since extended into industrial development, commercial renovation and architectural design. With active operations across Lagos, Abuja, Imo and Delta States, we bring local knowledge and national reach to every engagement.
