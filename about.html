Our Story

Incorporated in 2022 (RC No. 1793183), Cordon Blue Global Services Ltd built its reputation on critical CNG energy and telecom infrastructure — delivering stations for Gastech Nigeria and site works for MTN Nigeria. We have since expanded into industrial development, commercial renovation and architectural design.

Today we operate across Lagos, Abuja, Imo and Delta States, combining project management discipline with hands-on construction capability. We take ownership of outcomes — from first brief to final handover.
